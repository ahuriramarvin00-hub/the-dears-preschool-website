WHAT GUIDES US

Our mission, vision
& values.

At The Dears Pre-School, we work together to help every child learn, grow and feel included.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide exceptional care to children while fostering each child's intellectual, social, physical, and moral development in an academically rich environment.

🌍

Our Vision

To empower children to acquire, demonstrate and value knowledge and skills that will support them as lifelong learners, to participate in and contribute to the global world.

💙

Our Values

  • Respect
  • Tolerance and inclusion
  • Honesty
  • Teamwork
  • Appreciation
  • Excellence
